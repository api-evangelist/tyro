Tyro is an Australian fintech company providing integrated payment, banking and lending solutions for businesses. As one of Australia's largest EFTPOS providers outside the major banks, Tyro supports businesses across hospitality, retail, health, services, franchise and enterprise sectors with payment, banking and lending products.
